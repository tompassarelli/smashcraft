**Smashcraft 0.1.0 — a Warcraft III platform fighter**

Pick a Warcraft fighter, fight across floating stages, and knock your opponents out. Smashcraft brings platform fighting to Warcraft III, with 26 fighters, Classic and Definitive looks, computer opponents, Training, Classic routes and Lore Battles.

This is the first public version; it's early, expect rough edges, tell us what feels off. Feedback belongs in [the Smashcraft Discord feedback channel]. Please include your Warcraft version, fighters, stage, and what happened; a short clip helps.

**Controls:** keyboard play is available without extra software. The standard layout uses W/R to move left/right, E down, Space up, I to jump, Z to short hop, N to attack, U for specials and O to grab. Y opens pause. Check the in-game bindings for shield, directional attacks and your saved layout. Controller play uses the optional Smashcraft desktop app; [controller setup/download].
